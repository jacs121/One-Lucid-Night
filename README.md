# One Lucid Night

### DOWNLOAD:

_only windows is available for now_

* click on releases at the right
* open on the latest version
* click the executable for your system

OR

* under releases at the right click the latest version
* click the executable for your system

### STORY:

you go to sleep on a cold night
in your dream you where hiking in a forest,
you walk for awhile until you realized... this is a dream
and the moment that thought echoed, the world collapsed and your vision turns black.
as you come back your senses you realized:

> WHY AM I HOLDING A SHOTGUN??!!

### CONTROLS:

right click to shoot then <span style="color:red">__move your curser away from and towards the player to pump it__</span>
press R to reload or C to open the magazine if you need to see your ammo count

### GAME PLAY:

game loop: `after the tutorial the first wave will start, your mission is to survive the enemies but be worn they aren't easy to take on`

enemies: `styled around dream-core styled weird dreams`

items: `randomly spreading and giving you needed equipment or doing helpful actions`

runes: `randomly spreading powerful actionable power ups`

<details>
  <summary>reveal enemies</summary>

  Staticons: `a mix of a spider and a chameleon these enemies have a similar skin to the ground and thought moves when they move, looking straight at them will make them stop in place`

  Obeliskuses: `a very fast whale like enemy that will hume at you and move very fast until shot each will stop them for some time and make them slower`

  Maime (WIP, not yet spawnable): `small little creatures that disguise themselves has items, though are distinctive by having a slower passive animation`

  Diatum (future implementation): `slows you down and hurts you if you look at it`

  Feeterlug (future implementation): `walking on makes you unable to move or shoot any other enemies except it, you need to kill it and you cannot`
</details>

<details>
  <summary>reveal items</summary>
  
  ammo box: `gives you 8 bullets`

  medicine: `restores 10% of your health`

  full medicine kit: `restores 100% of your health`
</details>

<details>
  <summary>reveal runes</summary>
  
  Whisper rune (3 uses): `tells you how many enemies are left in the wave`

  harm rune (2 uses): `deals 8 damage to all enemies for every use it has`

  AdvancingRune (1 use): `appears at the end of a wave, used to start the next wave`
</details>
